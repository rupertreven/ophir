# Ophir — CFO Letter v1

> **Created:** 2026-09-28  
> **Last updated:** 2026-09-28  
> **Audience:** European CFO / Group Treasurer  
> **Purpose:** Explain what Ophir can deliver today.

## Dear CFO,

Financial markets are beginning a structural transition.

Money-market funds, deposits, bonds, private credit, receivables and collateral are increasingly becoming **tokenized, programmable and available through digital financial infrastructure**.

For a corporate CFO, however, the relevant question is not whether tokenization is interesting.

It is:

> **What can tokenization do for my company today — and is it actually worth it?**

That is what **Ophir** helps you determine and implement.

We are not a crypto exchange, custodian or investment fund. We work alongside your existing banks, ERP, accounting systems and regulated financial providers.

Today, Ophir can provide the following services.

## 1. Tokenization Opportunity Assessment

We analyze your treasury structure — cash, debt, working capital, currencies, collateral and funding requirements — and identify areas where regulated tokenized finance could create measurable value.

For every opportunity we compare the best conventional solution with the tokenized alternative.

The output is deliberately simple:

**NOW — PILOT — WATCH — NO BENEFIT.**

If conventional finance is better, we tell you.

## 2. Tokenized Cash & Liquidity

We assess whether corporate liquidity can benefit from regulated tokenized money-market funds, tokenized deposits or digital settlement assets.

We compare:

- net yield;
- liquidity and settlement;
- fees;
- tax;
- accounting treatment;
- custody;
- counterparty risk;
- operational requirements.

This includes access routes through regulated providers rather than requiring your organization to build wallets or blockchain infrastructure itself.

## 3. Tokenized Collateral

Corporate collateral can represent millions of euros of economically idle capital.

We assess whether regulated tokenized securities or money-market instruments can provide **yield while remaining usable as collateral**, and whether this is actually accepted by your financing counterparties.

We quantify the value after haircuts, custody costs, taxation and legal constraints.

## 4. Tokenized Corporate Funding

If your company is considering a new bond, private placement or commercial-paper programme, Ophir can evaluate a digitally native alternative.

For example:

> €50M bank/bond financing  
> versus  
> €50M DLT commercial paper or tokenized bond.

We assess potential differences in funding cost, issuance cost, settlement, investor access, denomination, administration and lifecycle management.

Where the business case works, we help structure the implementation with regulated issuance, custody and market-infrastructure partners.

## 5. Tokenized Working Capital

We assess whether receivables, supplier finance or other working-capital positions could benefit from digitally native financing.

The objective is not to tokenize invoices for its own sake.

The objective is to determine whether tokenization can provide **cheaper funding, faster access to liquidity, broader capital access or lower administrative friction**.

## 6. Digital FX & Cross-Border Settlement

For companies with material international flows, we analyze whether regulated digital-money rails can reduce:

- FX costs;
- prefunding;
- cash in transit;
- settlement delays;
- correspondent-banking friction.

Again, we benchmark this against your actual banking arrangements rather than against a theoretical legacy system.

## 7. Treasury Optimization Beyond Tokenization

Our analysis often discovers value that does not require blockchain at all.

Ophir can identify situations such as:

> cash earning 0.3% while another facility costs 4.5%;

> supplier discounts implying double-digit annualized returns;

> working capital unnecessarily trapped in receivables;

> avoidable external FX conversions between group entities.

These opportunities matter because tokenized assets compete for the **same corporate euro**.

Ophir therefore asks a broader question:

> **What is the highest-value permitted use of the next euro of corporate capital?**

Tokenization is included wherever it improves that answer.

## You do not need to become a crypto company

This is fundamental to our approach.

Ophir does not need to custody your assets or control your money.

Our model is:

> **Ophir analyzes and prepares.  
> Your company decides.  
> Regulated financial institutions execute.**

We translate the complexity between emerging digital markets and the systems your finance organization already understands.

That includes the financial business case, regulatory path, taxation, accounting consequences, provider selection and implementation.

Your ERP and accounting environment remain part of the process.

Your existing banks can remain part of the process.

And your CFO remains in control.

## A practical starting point

Our initial engagement is a **Tokenization Opportunity Assessment**.

We map your treasury and capital structure and produce a prioritized report showing:

**Current solution → Tokenized alternative → Financial benefit → Liquidity benefit → Risk → Tax → Accounting → Regulation → Implementation cost → Recommended next step.**

The objective is not to produce a blockchain strategy.

It is to answer a much more useful question:

> **Where can the new financial infrastructure make your company measurably better today?**

And where the answer is *nowhere yet*, we establish the baseline and continue monitoring the market as new regulated instruments and infrastructure become available.

# Ophir — CFO Letter v1

> **Created:** 2026-09-28  
> **Last updated:** 2026-10-01  
> **Audience:** Belgian CFO / Group Treasurer; broader European use cases remain research  
> **Purpose:** Explain the current assessment scope, conditional pilot and longer-term opportunity.

## Dear CFO,

Financial infrastructure is changing. Regulated funds and financial assets are becoming accessible through digital and, in some cases, tokenized rails.

For a corporate CFO, the relevant question is:

> **What can this infrastructure do for my company — and is it actually worth it?**

That is the question **Ophir** is built around.

Our ambition is to become *the corporate adoption layer for tokenized finance*: the intelligence and administrative layer connecting emerging financial infrastructure with corporate treasury.

Our first validation scope is deliberately narrower: **surplus EUR cash held by Belgian companies**. The current evidence consists of product specifications, regulatory research and instrument-level economic research. It does not yet establish a production-ready treasury platform or seven validated services.

We distinguish what can be assessed now from what requires a controlled pilot and what remains a market opportunity.

## NOW — A focused surplus-cash assessment

The practical starting point is a **Belgian Surplus-Cash Treasury Assessment**: a source-backed, analyst-led comparison using company-supplied balances, cash needs, liquidity constraints and actual bank quotes.

We examine:

- current-account remuneration and corporate notice/term deposits;
- conventional EUR money-market funds and short-duration alternatives;
- regulated tokenized EUR funds where access and product evidence can be established;
- yield, fees, risk, redemption conditions and time to usable EUR in your bank account;
- Belgian tax and accounting questions that could change the result.

The output is a factual scenario report with dated sources, explicit assumptions and missing evidence. It can establish a baseline and identify questions for your bank, accountant and prospective providers. It is not a personalised instruction to buy an instrument.

We compare against your actual banking arrangements and accessible conventional alternatives. Where comparable exposures exist on both rails, we separate the underlying investment return from any benefit of tokenization.

**Tokenization does not create the fund's yield.** Potential advantages such as easier reconciliation, wider settlement availability or reduced administration must be demonstrated for the specific instrument, provider and company.

### What the economics currently establish

Our economic research snapshot of **28 August 2026** illustrated a €2m surplus held for 92 days. Two Spiko funds showed approximately **€2.4k–€4.6k of gross incremental return** against one public Belgian term-deposit benchmark, assuming constant yields.

Those figures are historical illustrations, not current quotes, a comparison against your best bank offer, or a promise of future returns. The funds also have different risk profiles.

**Certified Net Treasury Benefit is not available today.** The exact Belgian treatment of the relevant share classes and account structures remains unconfirmed, including corporate tax, TOB, securities-account tax and accounting classification. These effects can erase a short-term gross advantage.

Until those questions are professionally validated, any after-tax scenario remains explicitly provisional. We cannot certify a final net benefit or claim that an instrument is economically superior. A commercial business case must also include Ophir's fees, implementation costs and evidence for any administrative savings.

## PILOT — Validate the treasury workflow end-to-end

The first product to validate is the Belgian surplus-cash workflow:

> **Cash baseline → liquidity policy → factual instrument comparison → company decision → regulated-provider execution → reconciliation and evidence.**

Spiko is the first reference adapter in the product plan, not an exclusive commitment or evidence of a completed integration.

A controlled pilot would test data imports or connections, cash calculations, provider handoff, post-trade reconciliation and accountant-review documentation. These are planned capabilities whose reliability and operational value still need to be demonstrated.

Before any live transaction or production recommendation, the relevant conditions must be met:

- professional validation of Belgian tax and accounting treatment for the exact instrument, share class and account;
- counsel review of the actual comparison, onboarding and handoff flow;
- confirmed corporate eligibility, provider responsibilities and required agreements;
- tested data, liquidity, approval, security and reconciliation controls;
- a customer-specific business case against conventional alternatives, including all material costs.

Research or a non-executing pilot may expose unresolved questions. It must not present them as validated tax outputs, filing-ready documents or certified savings. A live deployment remains blocked where a material condition is unresolved.

## WATCH — Broader corporate finance opportunities

These are research directions, **not currently available Ophir implementation services**:

| Opportunity | What must be proven before moving beyond WATCH |
|---|---|
| Yield-bearing tokenized collateral | Actual bank/counterparty acceptance, enforceable pledge/control, haircuts, liquidity and all-in Belgian economics. |
| Tokenized bonds / DLT commercial paper | Corporate access, real issuance and funding quotes, investor demand, licensed-party roles and a viable implementation path. |
| Tokenized receivables / supplier finance | An accessible regulated offering, legal treatment of claims, funding terms and measurable advantage over factoring, bank credit or conventional SCF. |
| Digital FX / cross-border settlement | Accessible provider rails, actual FX spreads and fees, prefunding needs, settlement-to-bank performance and regulatory responsibilities. |
| Tokenized deposits / programmable settlement | Availability to the target Belgian company, contractual rights, provider controls and demonstrated operational benefit. |
| Broader treasury optimization | Customer data and validated workflows for debt versus idle cash, supplier discounts, DSO and group netting; these are potential extensions beyond the first product scope. |

Institutional deployments and attractive arithmetic can justify research. They do not prove Belgian corporate access, an Ophir partnership or a customer benefit.

Where a conventional solution is better, that belongs in the conclusion.

## Clear status, clear control

Each assessment distinguishes:

- **NOW:** factual analysis supported by available evidence within the narrow current scope; no implication that an investment or integration is production-approved.
- **PILOT:** a defined hypothesis or workflow to test, with explicit conditions and unresolved items.
- **WATCH:** an opportunity that lacks sufficient access, evidence or implementation validation.
- **NO BENEFIT:** the comparison establishes no worthwhile incremental value at the stated assumptions.

An unknown result stays unknown. It is not converted into a positive recommendation.

Our intended operating model remains:

> **Ophir analyzes and prepares.  
> Your company decides.  
> Regulated financial institutions execute.**

Ophir's v1 design excludes custody of customer money or private keys, autonomous investment decisions and execution of investment orders. Provider handoff itself still requires validation of the exact legal and contractual boundary. Working with a regulated provider does not automatically validate Ophir's own activities.

Tax and accounting treatment require the appropriate professional review. Automated bank/ERP connections, journal exports and continuous monitoring are product goals to validate, rather than capabilities promised as operational today.

## A practical starting point

Start with one Belgian legal entity, one surplus-cash horizon and your actual bank terms.

The assessment should show:

**Current solution → Comparable alternative → Gross economics → Liquidity and risk → Costs → Tax/accounting validation status → Missing evidence → NOW / PILOT / WATCH / NO BENEFIT.**

Its value is a clearer decision: whether there is enough evidence to proceed to a controlled pilot, whether conventional finance already solves the problem, or whether more validation is needed.

> **A credible adoption case starts with an honest comparison — and earns the right to implementation.**

**Ophir**  
*Building the corporate adoption layer for tokenized finance.*

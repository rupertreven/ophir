## Belgian fiscal transparency — the catch

PwC Belgium states that foreign contractual mutual funds/FCPs are considered fiscally transparent in Belgium.

For Belgian corporate investors, annual tax reporting therefore requires a breakdown of income received by the fund as if the investor received it directly. The corporate breakdown must include at least:

- dividends;
- interest;
- capital gains;
- capital losses.

Carmignac itself warns Belgian investors that French FCP investors may need annual reporting of their share of fund income. Its retail explanation is not a corporate tax opinion, so corporate treatment requires separate professional validation.

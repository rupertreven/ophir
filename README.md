# Ophir

**The Treasury Operating System for the Tokenized Economy.**

Ophir is an AI-native European treasury infrastructure company being built from Belgium.

We believe capital is becoming programmable, financial assets are becoming tokenized, and corporate treasury will increasingly be operated by software and AI agents.

Ophir's goal is to give companies one intelligent control layer across banks, digital cash, tokenized assets, accounting systems and treasury workflows — without requiring finance teams to understand blockchains, wallets or smart contracts.

## Mission

**Build the operating system for digital capital.**

## Principles

- Treasury first, not crypto first.
- Non-custodial by default.
- Compliance by design.
- AI-native from day one.
- API-first infrastructure.
- Start small in Belgium; build for Europe and the world.
- Automate aggressively and keep the human organization exceptionally lean.

## Belgian fiscal transparency

PwC Belgium states that foreign contractual mutual funds are fiscally transparent in Belgium. Belgian corporate investors must include their attributable underlying fund income in annual tax reporting as if received directly. The corporate breakdown includes dividends, interest and capital gains/losses.

This means a simple 'NAV gain × 25% CIT' is only a **proxy**, not a filing-grade calculation. The exact underlying income ventilation determines the tax result.

Ophir opportunity: ingest the fund tax-reporting breakdown and automate Belgian corporate tax schedules, journal proposals and evidence packages.

## Annual securities-account tax (TACT)

From reference periods ending from 1 June 2026, Belgian TACT is 0.30% on securities accounts whose average taxable value exceeds EUR 1m. Legal persons are in scope, including foreign securities accounts for Belgian residents.

This tax applies to the **securities account**, not automatically to every fund holding. Therefore Ophir must establish the custody/access structure.

For a EUR 2m position held for only ~92 days, one cannot simply subtract EUR 6,000 (0.30% × EUR 2m). The statutory average account value over the reference period and valuation mechanics matter; depending on the account's other assets and observation dates, the average may or may not exceed EUR 1m. If units are not held on a qualifying securities account, the analysis may differ.
